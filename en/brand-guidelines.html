This page summarizes how to correctly use the name, logo, colors, and voice of Gamet Studios – for press, partners, subsidiary projects, and everyone inside the collective itself. The point isn't bureaucracy, it's consistency: no matter who designs something under our name, it should feel like Gamet Studios.

For anything not covered here, or for uses outside these guidelines: contact@gametstudios.media.

Name & Spelling

The name is always "Gamet Studios" – two words, both capitalized, no hyphen, never merged into one word.

  • Correct: Gamet Studios
  • Incorrect: GametStudios, Gamet-Studios, gamet studios, Gamet Studio (singular)

In running text, the name is treated as a single unit ("Gamet Studios is a creative collective…", not "…are a creative collective"). We're a collective, not a "company," an "agency," or a "studio network" – that word choice runs deliberately through all our copy.

Logo

Our logo comes in two color variants – a light one for dark backgrounds, a dark one for light backgrounds.

Both files are available for download in the press kit.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Always keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the "T" letter – no text, no other element, no image edge should come closer than that. In width, never render the logo smaller than about 60px, or the wordmark becomes hard to read.

Don't

  • Distort the proportions (squeezed, stretched, skewed)
  • Place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds without a darkening/lightening overlay
  • Recombine individual parts without checking with us first

Typography

Headings are set in Montserrat (bold, geometric), body copy in Inter (calm, readable at small sizes). Labels, dates, and small uppercase tags use Parkinsans.

Montserrat – for headings

Inter – for body copy and interface elements

Parkinsans – for logo labels

Voice & Tone

  • Always informal, first-name terms. We're a collective, not an institution.
  • Direct over bloated. Short sentences beat long, nested ones. Active verbs beat nominal style.
  • Collective, not company. "We" instead of "the company" or "the brand."
  • Cinematic where it fits – never forced. Vivid language is welcome when it describes something real, not as a stylistic flourish for its own sake.
  • No buzzwords. Words like "synergies," "state-of-the-art," or "holistic approach" don't appear in our copy.

In Short

Do: Use the logo unaltered, in one of the two color variants, with clear space. Use the accent color sparingly and deliberately. Speak directly, honestly, and as a collective.

Don't: Recolor the logo with low contrast, distort it, or add effects to it. Spell the name any other way than "Gamet Studios." Use corporate language or buzzwords.
